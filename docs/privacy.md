# Privacy

*Last updated: July 2026*

This site is the documentation for pypiron, published by Blackthorn Interstellar.
It is a static site with no accounts, no forms, and no data you submit to us.

## Advertising measurement

Pages on this site load an advertising pixel from OpenAI
(`oaiq`, served from `bzrcdn.openai.com`). It records that a page was viewed,
along with standard request information your browser sends — IP address,
user-agent, and the referring page. On pages with installation instructions, it
also records when you copy or select an install command. We use this for one
thing: measuring how our advertising performs and which pages bring people here.

OpenAI processes this data as described in its
[privacy policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/). We do not sell it,
and we do not combine it with any other information about you.

## Your choices

- Any content or tracker blocker (uBlock Origin, Privacy Badger, Brave shields,
  and similar) stops the pixel from loading.
- Your browser's "block third-party cookies" and privacy settings limit what it
  can record.
- **California residents:** you may opt out of the sharing of your personal
  information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Email
  [privacy@pypiron.com](mailto:privacy@pypiron.com) and we will honor the request.
