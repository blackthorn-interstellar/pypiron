# The blank-slate incremental reader — order instrument

Not a knowledge-inventory persona: a **protocol**. Every other actor holds
the whole page, so it has already seen the resolution of every forward
reference — cataphora is costless, buried ledes are invisible, and a
sentence that explains the link the reader just clicked reads as content
instead of as an echo. Confusion is a temporal phenomenon; this reader is
the only one that experiences time.

## What it catches that nothing else can
- Taglines announcing structure before content exists to fill it
  ("Two shapes, one rule:").
- Forward references that resolve eventually but cost the reader a held
  breath.
- Openers that restate the link text the reader arrived through.
- Missing information *at the moment the question forms* — even when the
  answer appears later on the page.

## Protocol (the mechanics are the instrument)
1. Split the passage into increments — phrase-level for the sentence under
   test, sentence-level around it — **one file per increment**.
2. The actor's entire world is the arrival context: the link text they
   clicked and the page title. Nothing else, no product knowledge.
3. Strict interleaving: read increment k, write the live reaction (2–4
   sentences, first person, visceral; "reads fine" is a valid reaction),
   THEN open increment k+1. The actor may never look ahead.
4. **Audit the transcript** before trusting any reaction: the tool-call
   sequence must alternate READ → reaction text → READ. A batch-read run is
   void — the reactions were written with the whole passage in context,
   which is exactly the blindness this instrument exists to remove.

## When to run it
New or rewritten ledes and openers — above all, copy authored while fixing
another finding: fix-time prose is written by someone holding the entire
page's context and never experienced in reading order by anyone, including
its author.
